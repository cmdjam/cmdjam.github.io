How it works

Drop thoughts into the Inbox as they arrive. When you have a moment, drag each one into a matrix quadrant and pick its project. Tap a task's title to rename it, the box to mark it done.

Tap a project chip to filter the matrix to just that project; tap it again, or All, to see everything. Drag the chips left and right to set priority — leftmost ranks highest, and in the all-projects view each quadrant is ordered by that ranking.

Export writes your whole desk to JSON; Import loads one back, for moving between devices. Everything auto-saves in this browser as you go.

The theme button switches between following your system's setting, light and dark.
